The problem

Property prices depend on dozens of interacting factors. The goal was a model that predicts price well — and an analysis that explains which factors matter most.

What I built

  • Cleaned historical real-estate data and engineered features with Pandas and NumPy.
  • Built and benchmarked Linear Regression and Random Forest models with Scikit-learn.
  • Achieved approximately 85–90% prediction accuracy on held-out test data.
  • Applied statistical analysis to identify the most important predictive variables.

How it was communicated

Results were presented through clear visualisations — predicted-vs-actual plots, residuals and feature importance — so that the findings make sense to someone who never opens the notebook.
